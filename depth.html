WEBXR / DEPTH-SENSING MODULE

True depth-dot scanning when supported.

This optional view requests estimated distances from your browser's AR depth provider and draws a field of green dots over the real world. Some Android browsers can calculate depth using camera movement without a LiDAR sensor.

DEVICE READINESS

CHECKING WEBXR SUPPORT…

These controls affect real browser-provided depth samples only if WebXR Depth Sensing succeeds. The scan cannot enable LiDAR hardware your phone doesn't contain.

The browser will request camera/spatial-tracking permissions. If your browser reports no AR depth support, use Dot Cloud on the main scanner instead. WebXR camera images are managed by the browser during AR.
